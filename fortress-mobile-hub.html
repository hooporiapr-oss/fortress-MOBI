The Stations
STATION 1
THE RECALL
Watch a pattern flash all at once, then repeat it back in order.
🏀 En la cancha: Ver la jugada una vez y recordarla; leer la formación de un vistazo.On the court: See a play once and remember it; read the formation at a glance.
STATION 2
THE RECOGNITION
Watch the original, then spot what changed in the echo that follows.
🏀 En la cancha: Notar el cambio de la defensa, la ayuda o la rotación antes que nadie.On the court: Catch the defensive switch, the help or the rotation before anyone else.
STATION 3
THE REPLAY
The chain keeps everything you have learned and adds one more cell every round — and each cell has its own note, so it climbs as it grows.
🏀 En la cancha: Ejecutar jugadas de varios pasos y aplicar el scouting sin olvidar nada.On the court: Run multi-step plays and carry out the scouting report without missing a thing.
STATION 4
THE FORTRESS
Watch a sequence of targets light up, then repeat it exactly. Every round adds one more — how far can you go?
🏀 En la cancha: Decidir bien en el cierre del partido, cansado y con presión.On the court: Make the right call late in the game, tired and under pressure.
